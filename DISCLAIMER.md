# Disclaimer

## Research Use Only

This project was developed as a Bachelor's Semester Project (BSP S2) at the University of Luxembourg. Everything in this repository -- the code, evaluation pipeline, and any outputs -- is strictly for academic research. It should not be interpreted as a finished product or a clinically validated system.

## Not Medical Advice

The outputs generated by the models in this pipeline -- differential diagnoses, treatment recommendations, drug suggestions, safety flags, confidence scores -- **do not constitute medical advice** in any form.

Large language models can and do produce incorrect, outdated, or dangerous medical information. The models evaluated here have not been reviewed by clinical experts or approved by any regulatory body. They have no access to the full clinical context of any real patient.

**Do not use this system, or any outputs derived from it, to make health decisions.** Always consult a qualified medical professional.

## Data

This project uses the **MIMIC-IV v3.1** clinical database (Johnson et al., PhysioNet, 2024). Access was obtained through PhysioNet's credentialed access program, following completion of CITI human subjects training and acceptance of the data use agreement.

**This repository does not contain any patient data.** Raw MIMIC-IV files, the local DuckDB database, and any exported CSVs with patient-level records are excluded by `.gitignore`. All reported results are aggregated across the full cohort.

Anyone who wishes to reproduce this work must independently obtain MIMIC-IV access through PhysioNet and must comply with the PhysioNet Data Use Agreement and applicable data protection regulations (including HIPAA where applicable).

## Aggregated Results

All quantitative results in the paper and dashboard (BLEU/ROUGE scores, evaluator scores, win rates, LIME weights) represent aggregates or medians across 2,000 patients. No individual patient's data, responses, or identifiers appear anywhere in this repository.
